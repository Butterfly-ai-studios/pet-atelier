Support

Most issues are setup, not bugs. Work through the checklist below first — it fixes the majority of reports in minutes.

Before you write in

  • Check the documentation for the section you're stuck on.
  • Note your theme version (Theme settings → theme info) and the exact steps that reproduce the problem.
  • If a section looks empty, check its settings first: most sections need a collection, a blog or a menu picked before they show anything.

Common fixes

The Shop dropdown shows no descriptions

Descriptions come from the collections themselves. Edit each collection's description in Products → Collections and the menu updates automatically.

The finder shows "No matches yet"

Either the final answer's collection is empty, or no fallback collection is set. Set a fallback collection in the finder section, and make sure the collections linked from the final answers actually contain products.

Care Plan cards don't appear on a product

Cards only appear when the product has selling plans from Shopify Subscriptions or your subscription app. The theme never invents plans. Check the product's selling plans in admin first.

The 3D viewer doesn't appear

The viewer only loads on products that have a 3D model (GLB) attached as product media. Attach one under Products → the product → Media → Add media → 3D model, and wait for its status to read Ready.

Pet profiles don't show on the account page

Profiles come from the customer metafield custom.pet_profiles (JSON). See the documentation's My Pets section for the exact format.

Something looks off after an update

Re-publish the theme, then hard-refresh the storefront (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + R). If the problem survives a hard refresh, note the browser and device and contact us below.

Contact the theme team

Use the support link on the Theme Store listing for Pet Atelier — messages there reach the theme team directly. Or email us at support@vooqio.com. Include your store URL, theme version, and the steps to reproduce the issue, and we'll get back to you.
